## Result

I found a small number of genuine “ran it, broke it, changed the harness” logs. The strongest are personal production harnesses—not vendor memory products. Most Letta/mem0/Zep-style material found was architecture/product guidance rather than an account of a specific system improving over months. That absence is meaningful.

### What is most worth copying into `pi`

1. **Promotion gates, not autonomous rule writing.** The strongest real log requires repeated evidence across sessions before an inferred lesson becomes policy; direct user corrections bypass the gate. [Source](https://www.practicalsystems.io/blog/seven-months-of-ai-agent-memory-what-rotted-what-survived)

2. **A deterministic capture hook plus asynchronous consolidation.** Session termination should queue raw evidence; a later job should decide what belongs in durable memory. This avoids depending on a task-focused model to remember to journal. [Source](https://anandbg.com/blog/hermes-agent-claude-code-native)

3. **Store lessons as evidence, not imperatives.** “When X broke, Y fixed it” is safer than “always do Y,” especially when agents read untrusted text. [Source](https://www.practicalsystems.io/blog/seven-months-of-ai-agent-memory-what-rotted-what-survived)

4. **Make every durable claim attributable and expirable.** The mature log’s `[stated]` / `[observed]` / `[inferred]` distinction, plus `stale_after`-style checking, is more defensible than a magical vector memory bucket. [Source](https://www.practicalsystems.io/blog/seven-months-of-ai-agent-memory-what-rotted-what-survived)

5. **Promote repeatable procedures into hooks/tests, not merely `AGENTS.md`.** The actual failure-to-enforcement loop is the most convincing “self-improvement” mechanism found. [Source](https://www.practicalsystems.io/blog/my-claude-code-harness-hooks-skills-rules-that-became-code)

### Excluded as insufficiently evidenced

- Mem0’s [“retrieve, reason, writeback” post](https://mem0.ai/blog/how-to-create-ai-agents-with-long-term-memory) describes a “typical production” loop, but I found no specific deployment log, outcome history, or failure account in it. **UNVERIFIED as a real customer build log.**
- General self-improving-agent tutorials and reflection examples were excluded when they presented runnable patterns without evidence that the author operated them across sessions in real work.
